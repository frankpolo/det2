Know your options

Understanding dentures

Dentures are custom-made replacements for missing teeth and surrounding tissues. They restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence — and you'll look as natural as ever.

Modern dentures are lighter, more comfortable, and more lifelike than ever before. At Krishna Dental, we craft each set with precision using premium materials, so they fit perfectly and feel like your own teeth.

Personalized solutions

Types of dentures

Complete Dentures

Replace all upper or lower teeth. Custom-crafted to match your jaw shape and face, they restore full function and appearance.

Partial Dentures

Replace some missing teeth while preserving your natural ones. They attach to remaining teeth for stability and natural chewing.

Flexible Dentures

Made with thermoplastic materials that are more flexible and comfortable. They're easier to insert and remove, and less likely to break.

Implant-Supported

Anchored to dental implants for superior stability. They don't move or slip, and feel even more like natural teeth.

Your journey

How we create your dentures

01

Consultation

We discuss your needs, examine your mouth, and assess your bone structure. We help you choose the best type of dentures for your situation and budget.

02

Impressions

We take detailed impressions and measurements of your mouth. These precise records are used to create dentures that fit perfectly and function naturally.

03

Try-in

We show you a pre-final version to check fit, comfort, and appearance. Any adjustments are made at this stage to ensure they look and feel just right.

04

Fitting & delivery

Your final dentures are inserted, and we make final adjustments. We show you how to insert, remove, and care for them properly.

05

Adjustments & follow-up

We schedule follow-up visits to make fine adjustments and ensure your comfort. Your gums may change over time, and we'll help keep your dentures fitting perfectly.
