Root Canal Treatment

No, modern root canal treatments at Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada are designed to be completely pain-free. Led by our MDS specialists, we use advanced localized anesthetics and computer-guided rotary endodontics to ensure absolute comfort. The procedure is actually performed to immediately relieve the severe throbbing pain caused by a deep tooth infection, rather than causing pain.
At our Gudivada clinic, a root canal typically takes 1 to 2 visits. For standard pulp infections, Dr. Vidya can perform a single-sitting root canal in under 60 minutes. However, if there is severe swelling, active abscesses, or complex canal anatomy, we schedule a second appointment to place clinical-grade sterile dressings inside the tooth to guarantee long-term treatment success before sealing it.
Yes, placing a custom dental crown (cap) is vital to preserve the tooth structure after a root canal. Because the procedure removes the infected blood and nerve supply, the tooth becomes dry and fragile over time. At Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada, we design custom Zirconia and CAD/CAM porcelain crowns that act as a protective shield, preventing fractures and restoring 100% chewing function.
A single-visit root canal at Krishna Dental in Gudivada is completed in one session and is recommended for uncomplicated tooth decay without active inflammation. If the tooth has severe swelling, a multi-visit root canal is performed; we clean the root canals and place a temporary sanitizing medicine inside the tooth for 7 to 10 days to eliminate bacteria before final obturation.
Though rare, a root canal tooth can develop a secondary infection due to new decay, structural fractures, or a saliva leak. At Krishna Multispeciality Dental Hospital in Gudivada, we resolve these issues using advanced endodontic retreatment, which cleans and reseals the complex micro-canals to save your natural tooth.
Common symptoms indicating a deep pulp infection include persistent throbbing pain, sensitivity to hot and cold that lingers for minutes, discomfort when biting, swollen gums around the affected area, or a visible pimple-like bump on the gum line. If you notice these signs, our specialists in Gudivada recommend scheduling a digital X-ray evaluation immediately.

Dental Implants

A dental implant is a permanent, medical-grade titanium post placed surgically into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. Through osseointegration, the implant fuses naturally with the bone over a few months. Dr. Vidya Sahithi (MDS) then secures a custom CAD/CAM porcelain or zirconia crown on top, providing a tooth replacement that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.
Yes, dental implants are extremely safe and are considered the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. When placed by experienced MDS implantologists using our clinic's highly sterile operatory protocols in Gudivada, dental implants have a clinical success rate of over 95% to 98%.
At Krishna Dental Hospital, placing the titanium implant post is a minor, comfortable procedure taking under 45 minutes under local anesthesia. The entire process takes 3 to 6 months: we allow 3 to 4 months for the bone to fuse securely around the implant, followed by taking digital impressions to fabricate and place your final custom crown.
A bone graft is only required if your jawbone lacks the thickness or volume to support an implant, which often happens after long-term tooth loss or severe gum disease. During your consultation at our Gudivada facility, we use a 3D CBCT scan to evaluate your bone density. If needed, a minor bone grafting procedure is performed to create a solid foundation for the implant.
You care for dental implants just like natural teeth. We recommend brushing twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush, flossing daily (using implant-specific floss or a water flosser), and visiting Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada twice a year for routine professional cleanings to maintain healthy surrounding gums.
A dental implant is a standalone replacement that preserves neighboring teeth and stimulates the jawbone to prevent bone loss. A dental bridge requires grinding down and capping the healthy neighboring teeth to suspend a fake tooth in the middle. Implants are more durable, easier to clean, and offer a longer-term solution for patients in Gudivada.

Orthodontics (Braces & Aligners)

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. At Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada, our specialists assess emerging permanent teeth and jaw growth early, allowing us to detect bite discrepancies and implement preventive or interceptive treatments when needed.
Traditional braces use metal brackets and wires to guide teeth into position and cannot be removed by the patient. Clear aligners (such as Invisalign) are customized, transparent medical-grade plastic trays that are virtually invisible and removable for eating, brushing, and flossing. We offer both advanced systems at our Gudivada practice.
Active orthodontic treatment typically lasts between 12 to 24 months, depending on your individual bite complexity. During your initial orthodontic consultation at Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada, we use digital scans to map out your treatment timeline and show you how your teeth will move.
Bonding orthodontic brackets to your teeth is a completely painless procedure. As your teeth begin to move during the first 3 to 5 days after placement or adjustments, you may feel mild soreness or pressure. At our Gudivada clinic, we provide detailed soft-food guides and comfort instructions to help minimize this temporary adjustment phase.
To prevent damaging brackets or bending wires, patients wearing traditional braces should avoid hard, sticky, or highly crunchy foods. This includes popcorn, nuts, sticky candies, and chewing gum. Hard fruits like apples should be sliced into small pieces rather than bitten into directly.
Yes, wearing a retainer is essential to maintain your new smile. Teeth have a natural tendency to slowly shift back toward their original positions over time. At Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada, we provide custom retainers and long-term retention plans to keep your teeth permanently stabilized in their optimal alignment.

Smile Makeover & Teeth Whitening

A digital smile makeover is a customized aesthetic plan designed to enhance your smile symmetry and facial harmony. Using digital dental design, Dr. Vidya combines cosmetic treatments such as thin porcelain veneers, dental crowns, teeth whitening, and gum contouring to transform your smile's shape, color, and alignment.
A porcelain veneer is a thin ceramic shell bonded only to the visible front surface of a tooth, requiring minimal preparation, to correct aesthetic concerns like minor chips or gaps. A dental crown caps the entire tooth structure to restore strength, commonly used for teeth weakened by large cavities or root canals.
Yes, professional teeth whitening at Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada is completely safe for your enamel. Under clinical supervision, we use neutral-pH whitening gels and specialized LED activation lights that lift deep stains without damaging your tooth structure or causing long-term sensitivity.
Professional teeth whitening results generally last between 1 to 3 years. The longevity of your bright smile depends on dietary habits. To maintain results, we advise limiting highly pigmented beverages like tea and coffee, avoiding tobacco products, and maintaining regular preventive cleanings.
No, high-quality porcelain veneers are highly resistant to staining. Porcelain is non-porous, meaning pigments from coffee, tea, or food do not penetrate the surface. However, to keep your gums healthy and maintain the bonding resin, regular checkups and cleanings at our Gudivada clinic are recommended.
Composite bonding is a quick, minimally invasive cosmetic procedure where a tooth-colored composite resin is applied and shaped to repair minor chips, cracks, or spacing. Completed in a single visit at Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada, bonding results last 5 to 7 years with good home care and hygiene.

General Dentistry & Oral Hygiene

We recommend getting a professional dental scaling and cleaning every 6 months to prevent plaque accumulation. For patients with a history of gum disease, deep periodontal pockets, or rapid tartar buildup, our team in Gudivada may recommend maintenance cleanings every 3 to 4 months.
No, professional scaling does not loosen teeth. Scaling uses gentle ultrasonic vibrations and water flow to remove calculus (tartar) that regular brushing cannot clean. Removing this buildup might cause temporary temperature sensitivity for a few days, but it is essential to prevent chronic bone loss and gum disease.
Gums bleed due to plaque buildup along the gumline, which causes inflammation (gingivitis). With regular flossing, soft brushing, and a professional scaling appointment at Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada, gingivitis can be completely reversed. If bleeding continues, it is important to schedule an evaluation.
Plaque is a soft, sticky film of bacteria that constantly forms on your teeth and can be brushed away daily. If not removed within 24 to 48 hours, plaque hardens into tartar (calculus). Tartar cannot be removed by brushing and requires professional scaling tools at our Gudivada clinic.
We recommend scheduling your child's first dental visit when their first tooth appears, or by their first birthday. Early visits at Krishna Dental Hospital in Gudivada focus on monitoring jaw development, guiding parents on infant oral care, and building a positive, comfortable relationship with the dentist.
Pit and fissure sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the deep chewing grooves of a child's permanent molars. The sealant bonds to the tooth, acting as a physical barrier that prevents food particles and cavity-causing bacteria from getting trapped in hard-to-clean grooves.
Treating bad breath starts with diagnosing its cause, which is often bacterial plaque, untreated cavities, gum inflammation, or dry mouth. A professional scaling and dental exam at our Gudivada clinic, combined with targeted hygiene routines, can effectively eliminate oral bacteria and restore fresh breath.
