Our story

Built on trust, driven by care

At Krishna Dental, we believe that every patient deserves a dentist they can trust. Led by Dr. Vidya Sahithi Atluri (MDS), we started with a simple mission: to bring compassionate, honest dental care to our community in Gudivada and the surrounding Krishna district.

We treat every patient like family — listening to your concerns, explaining your options clearly, and recommending only the treatments you truly need. No pressure. No surprises. Just good medicine delivered with genuine care.

Over the years, we've invested in the latest technology — from digital X-rays to advanced materials — because we believe modern dentistry should be better, faster, and more comfortable for you. Read more about Dr. Vidya's clinical credentials and specialist qualifications.
