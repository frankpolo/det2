Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Information We Collect

At Krishna Multispeciality Dental Clinic, we collect information to provide you with excellent dental care and services. Here's what we collect:

  • Contact Information: When you book an appointment or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Medical Information: During your visit, we collect relevant dental and medical history to provide appropriate treatment.
  • Payment Information: We process payments for services, but we do not store credit card or banking details on our servers. Payment processing is handled by secure third-party payment processors.
  • Analytics: We use basic analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as pages viewed and visit duration. This helps us improve our website experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for specific, legitimate purposes:

  • Appointment Scheduling: Your contact information is used to confirm appointments and send appointment reminders.
  • Treatment Planning: Medical and dental history helps our dentists plan the best treatment for your needs.
  • Follow-up Communications: We may send treatment reminders, post-care instructions, or information about preventive care.
  • Service Improvement: We review patient feedback and aggregate data to enhance our services, clinic facilities, and patient experience.
  • Legal Compliance: We maintain records as required by medical and dental regulations in India.

Information Sharing

Your privacy is important to us. We have strict policies about sharing your information:

  • Never Sold: We never sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Treatment Team Only: Your information is shared only with our dental team members and support staff directly involved in your care.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or a valid court order, we may disclose information to the appropriate authorities.
  • No Marketing Third Parties: We do not share your information with marketing companies or external advertisers.

Data Security

We implement multiple measures to protect your personal and medical information:

  • Secure Forms: All online booking and contact forms use SSL encryption (HTTPS) to protect data during transmission.
  • Limited Access: Only authorized clinic staff can access patient records and personal information.
  • Regular Review: We regularly review our security practices and update them to meet current standards.
  • Physical Security: Patient records and files are stored securely in locked areas within our clinic.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Like most websites, we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Analytics Cookies Only: We use basic analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors navigate our site, which pages are most useful, and where improvements can be made.
  • No Advertising Trackers: We do not use advertising or tracking cookies that follow you across other websites.
  • Your Control: You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.

Your Rights

You have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request to see what personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correct: If any of your information is inaccurate or incomplete, you can ask us to correct it.
  • Right to Delete: Subject to legal retention requirements, you can request deletion of your personal information. We'll comply where legally permitted.
  • How to Exercise Your Rights: Contact us at vidya@krishnadental.in with your request. We'll respond within 30 days.
