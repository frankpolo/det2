Common Dental Emergencies We Handle

Severe Toothache Pain

Excruciating throbbing pain that wakes you up at night is often a sign of pulp infection. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll prioritize you for a same-day, pain-free emergency root canal as soon as we open.

Knocked-Out or Loose Tooth

Accidental trauma? If a tooth has been knocked out, clean it gently, keep it moist in milk or saliva, and visit our clinic within 60 minutes to save the tooth.

Broken, Cracked or Chipped Teeth

A broken tooth or lost crown exposes sensitive nerves, leading to intense pain and risks of infection. We provide same-day restorations, crowns, and bonding.

Swollen Gums & Abscesses

A dental abscess is a serious bacterial infection that creates painful swellings. Immediate clinical drainage and antibiotic therapy are crucial to prevent systemic spread.

🦷 What to Expect During an Emergency Visit

When you arrive with an acute dental emergency, our clinical protocol is optimized for speed and maximum comfort:

  • Rapid Diagnostics: An immediate low-radiation digital X-ray is taken to diagnose the root cause within minutes.
  • Pain Management: We utilize advanced local anaesthetics and pain relief protocols to ensure any immediate treatment is painless.
  • Clear Options & Transparency: Before starting any procedure, we explain the findings and detail the costs transparently.

Our priority is to get you out of active pain and stabilize the condition to prevent further complications.

💡 Dental Emergency Guidelines (While On Your Way)

  • For Severe Pain: Rinse with warm salt water. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gums, as this causes tissue burns.
  • For a Knocked-Out Tooth: Handle only by the chewing crown. Keep the tooth submerged in milk or a balanced salt solution.
  • For Swelling: Apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek for 10-minute intervals.
Clinical Interventions

Immediate Relief & Treatment for Dental Trauma

When sudden dental trauma or severe infection strikes, immediate and precise clinical intervention is necessary to save your natural teeth and prevent oral health decay. At Krishna Dental, we provide dedicated same-day solutions:

  • Emergency Pulpectomy & Root Canals: If decay or a deep fracture reaches the pulp chamber, we perform immediate pulpectomy to remove infected nerve tissue and instantly stop throbbing pain.
  • Dental Abscess Drainage: Advanced periodontal or periapical infections can cause severe local swellings. We perform sterile drainage and localized disinfection to stop bacteria from entering your bloodstream.
  • Splinting & Re-implantation: For loose or avulsed teeth due to physical trauma, we perform composite or wire splinting to stabilize the teeth and allow the bone and periodontal ligaments to heal.

First aid while you're on your way

For a knocked-out tooth: pick it up by the crown (never the root), rinse it gently in milk or saline if dirty, and try to place it back in the socket if possible — or keep it in milk if not. Time matters here; a tooth has the best chance of being saved within 30–60 minutes.

For severe toothache: rinse with warm salt water and take an over-the-counter pain reliever, but avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum, as this can burn the tissue. For a broken or chipped tooth, save any pieces if you can, rinse your mouth gently, and apply a cold compress to reduce swelling on the way to us.

Not every dental problem that feels urgent needs an emergency visit the same day, and it helps to know the difference. Sharp, sudden, or worsening pain, visible swelling, a knocked-out or badly broken tooth, or any injury involving heavy bleeding all warrant contacting us straight away. A dull ache that comes and goes, or minor sensitivity, can usually wait for a regular appointment — though it's always fine to call and describe what you're experiencing if you're unsure which category it falls into. Our team would rather you call and ask than sit in pain wondering whether it's serious enough to bother us.
