How to Reach Krishna Dental from Eluru

1. Start from Eluru. Head toward Gudivada via NH16 (also called Chennai–Vijayawada National Highway). This is the primary route connecting Eluru to Gudivada.

4. Find the clinic landmark. Look for the MedPlus pharmacy — our clinic is right beside it. You'll also see the Homeopathy College nearby. Ample parking is available.
