Getting Here

How to Reach Krishna Dental from Kaikaluru Region

From Kaikaluru, take the Gudivada-Kaikaluru Road heading south toward Gudivada (approximately 25 km, 30-minute drive). Enter Gudivada town and continue straight on Eluru Road. Our clinic is on the right side, beside MedPlus and opposite Homeopathy College.

By Bus: Direct APSRTC buses run from Kaikaluru bus stand to Gudivada every 20-30 minutes. Get off at the Gudivada bus stand and take an auto to Eluru Road (INR 30-40).

From Villages: Buses from Kanukollu, Chintalapudi, Putlacheruvu, Lellapudi, and other villages connect to Kaikaluru first, then onward to Gudivada. Total journey typically under 45 minutes.
