What is a smile makeover?

A smile makeover is a personalized combination of cosmetic and functional dental treatments designed to transform your entire smile. Rather than fixing one tooth, we look at your smile as a whole — the shape, colour, spacing, and alignment of all your visible teeth.

The beauty is in the planning. Using advanced digital imaging, we create a custom mockup of your new smile tailored to your face shape and features. You approve the design before we begin, so there are no surprises. We combine treatments like veneers, whitening, crowns, and orthodontics to achieve the exact look you want.

Your smile, transformed

Treatments we combine

Veneers

Thin, custom porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth to change their shape, size, and colour instantly.

Professional Whitening

Brighten dull or stained teeth several shades with our advanced in-office whitening — faster and safer than over-the-counter options.

Dental Crowns

Custom-made caps that cover damaged or weak teeth, restoring their shape, strength, and appearance for a natural look.

Orthodontics

Straighten crooked or crowded teeth using braces or clear aligners to improve both appearance and bite alignment.

Step by step

The smile design process

01

Digital photography

We take detailed photographs of your smile from multiple angles using professional lighting. These images form the foundation for your digital design.

02

Smile analysis

Our cosmetic specialists analyze your smile against your facial proportions, skin tone, and natural features. We identify areas to enhance and create a vision for your ideal smile.

03

Digital mockup & preview

Using advanced imaging software, we create a digital mockup showing exactly how your new smile will look. You see it before we start — and we refine it until you love it.

04

Treatment execution

We follow your approved design precisely, combining the necessary treatments — veneers, whitening, crowns, straightening — in the right sequence to achieve your vision.

05

Final reveal

Your makeover is complete. We take final photos to compare with your original smile and show you the stunning transformation you've achieved.
