Professional Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is one of the most popular cosmetic dental treatments available today. It's a simple, non-invasive way to enhance your smile by lightening the color of your teeth and removing stains and discoloration.

At Krishna Multispeciality Dental Clinic in Gudivada, we offer professional teeth whitening treatments that are safe, effective, and deliver remarkable results that over-the-counter products simply can't match.

Why Choose Professional Teeth Whitening?

While there are many over-the-counter whitening products available, professional teeth whitening performed by our dental professionals offers several advantages:

  • Faster results - Professional treatments can whiten teeth by several shades in just one session
  • More effective - Professional-strength whitening agents provide superior results
  • Safer application - Dentist supervision ensures proper protection of gums and soft tissues
  • Customized treatment - We tailor the whitening process to your specific needs
  • Long-lasting results - Professional whitening tends to last longer than at-home alternatives

Our Teeth Whitening Options

At Krishna Multispeciality Dental Clinic, we offer two primary teeth whitening options:

In-Office Whitening

Our in-office whitening procedure is the quickest way to whiten your teeth. The treatment typically takes about 60-90 minutes and can make your teeth several shades whiter in just one visit. Here's what to expect:

  1. Our dental team will clean your teeth and examine your oral health to ensure you're a good candidate for whitening.
  2. We'll apply a protective barrier to your gums to prevent irritation.
  3. A professional-strength whitening gel is applied to your teeth.
  4. In some cases, a special light or laser may be used to enhance the whitening process.
  5. The gel is removed after a specific amount of time, and the process may be repeated multiple times during the same session.
  6. After the final application, we'll rinse your mouth and apply a post-treatment fluoride to help reduce sensitivity.

Take-Home Whitening Kits

For those who prefer to whiten their teeth at their own pace, we offer custom-made take-home whitening trays. These are more effective than over-the-counter products because:

  • The trays are custom-fitted to your teeth for better whitening agent coverage
  • We provide professional-strength whitening gel that's not available over the counter
  • You receive proper instructions for safe and effective use
  • The treatment can be adjusted based on your sensitivity and whitening goals

With take-home kits, you'll typically wear the trays for 30 minutes to a few hours each day, over a period of 1-2 weeks, depending on your whitening goals and our dentist's recommendations.

Common Causes of Tooth Discoloration

Understanding what causes tooth discoloration can help you maintain your whitening results longer. Common causes include:

Extrinsic Stains (Surface Stains)

  • Dark-colored beverages such as coffee, tea, red wine, and cola
  • Foods with strong colors such as berries, soy sauce, and tomato-based sauces
  • Tobacco use (smoking or chewing)
  • Poor oral hygiene that allows plaque and food stains to build up

Intrinsic Stains (Internal Stains)

  • Certain medications like tetracycline antibiotics
  • Excessive fluoride exposure during tooth development
  • Trauma to teeth causing internal bleeding and discoloration
  • Aging, which naturally makes teeth more yellow over time
  • Genetics, which influences your natural tooth color

Maintaining Your Whitening Results

To keep your teeth bright and white after your professional whitening treatment, we recommend:

  • Avoiding or limiting foods and beverages that can stain teeth
  • Drinking staining beverages through a straw to minimize contact with teeth
  • Rinsing your mouth with water after consuming staining foods or drinks
  • Brushing teeth twice daily and flossing once a day
  • Using whitening toothpaste to help remove surface stains
  • Getting regular dental cleanings
  • Scheduling periodic touch-up whitening treatments as needed

Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

While teeth whitening is safe for most people, it may not be suitable for everyone. It's best to consult with our dentist before undergoing whitening treatment if you:

  • Have sensitive teeth or gums
  • Have dental restorations such as crowns, veneers, or fillings on front teeth
  • Have very dark stains that may not respond well to whitening
  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have teeth that are gray in color (which may indicate dead tooth nerves)
