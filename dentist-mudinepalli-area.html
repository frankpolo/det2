Getting Here

How to Reach Krishna Dental from Mudinepalli Region

By Road (Recommended): From Mudinepalli, take the Gudivada-Mudinepalli Road heading north (15 km, 20-minute drive). From Gudlavalleru (20 km) and Pamarru (20 km), take the road toward Gudivada. Enter Gudivada and continue straight on Eluru Road. Krishna Dental is on your right, beside MedPlus pharmacy and near the Homeopathy College.

By Bus: Frequent APSRTC buses run from Mudinepalli, Gudlavalleru, Pamarru, and Nandivada to Gudivada bus stand (every 15-20 minutes). Get off at the Gudivada bus stand and take an auto to "Krishna Dental near MedPlus on Eluru Road" — 5 minutes, about INR 30-40.

From Smaller Villages: If you're coming from Alluru, Chevuru, Kakaravada, or other interior villages, catch a bus to the nearest town (Mudinepalli or Gudlavalleru) and connect to the frequent Gudivada service.

Return Same Day: Most treatments allow same-day return. Book an early morning slot and you'll be home by lunch. Free parking available for those driving.
