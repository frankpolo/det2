Dr. Vidya Sahithi Atluri

MDS Prosthodontist

Dr. Vidya Sahithi Atluri is the founder and lead dentist at Krishna Multispeciality Dental Clinic in Gudivada. With her advanced MDS degree in Prosthodontics, she is the only specialist-level prosthodontist practicing in the Gudivada region — bringing expertise in dental implants, full mouth rehabilitation, and cosmetic dentistry that was previously only available in larger cities like Vijayawada.

Her philosophy is simple: provide world-class dental care that is accessible, affordable, and delivered with genuine compassion. Every patient is treated with the same care she would give her own family.

Why It Matters

Why Choose an MDS Prosthodontist?

A Prosthodontist is a dentist with 3+ years of advanced specialty training beyond dental school, focused exclusively on restoring and replacing teeth. While a general dentist handles routine care, a prosthodontist is the expert for complex cases involving implants, full mouth rehabilitation, and cosmetic transformations.

Dr. Vidya is the only MDS Prosthodontist practicing in the Gudivada region. This means:

  • Advanced implant surgery — single to full arch cases
  • Full mouth rehabilitation — complex cases general dentists refer out
  • Cosmetic smile design — artistic + scientific approach
  • Same-day crowns — with advanced materials and techniques
  • Comprehensive treatment planning — coordinated care for complex cases
