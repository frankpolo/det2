What is a Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment (endodontic treatment) is a dental procedure designed to eliminate bacteria from the infected root canal, prevent reinfection of the tooth and save the natural tooth. During the procedure, the inflamed or infected pulp is removed, and the inside of the tooth is carefully cleaned and disinfected, then filled and sealed.

At Krishna Multispeciality Dental Clinic in Gudivada, our experienced dentists use modern techniques to ensure that your root canal treatment is as comfortable and painless as possible.

When Do You Need a Root Canal?

You may need a root canal if you experience any of the following symptoms:

  • Severe pain while chewing or applying pressure
  • Prolonged sensitivity to heat or cold
  • Darkening or discoloration of the tooth
  • Swelling and tenderness in nearby gums
  • Persistent pimple on the gums
  • Deep decay or a crack in the tooth that has allowed bacteria to reach the pulp

The Root Canal Procedure

A root canal procedure typically involves the following steps:

Step 1: Examination and X-ray

First, our dentist will examine your tooth and take X-rays to determine the extent of the infection and to check the shape of the root canals.

Step 2: Anesthesia

Local anesthesia is administered to numb the area around the affected tooth, ensuring that you feel no pain during the procedure.

Step 3: Pulp Removal

A small access hole is drilled into the tooth to remove the diseased and infected pulp. Using specialized tools called files, the dentist cleans out the pulp chamber and root canals.

Step 4: Cleaning and Shaping

The root canals are thoroughly cleaned, shaped, and disinfected using irrigation solutions to ensure all infection is removed.

Step 5: Filling and Sealing

Once cleaned, the root canals are filled with a rubber-like material called gutta-percha and sealed with adhesive cement to ensure complete closure of the canals.

Step 6: Restoration

After the root canal, a crown or other restoration is usually placed on the tooth to protect it, restore its functionality, and prevent future damage.

Myths and Facts About Root Canal Treatment

Myth: Root canal treatment is painful

Fact: With modern techniques and effective anesthesia, root canal treatment is no more painful than getting a filling. Most patients report being comfortable during the procedure.

Myth: It's better to extract a tooth than have a root canal

Fact: Saving your natural tooth through root canal treatment is almost always the best option. Natural teeth are stronger, more efficient for chewing, and help maintain jawbone structure.

Myth: Root canal treatment causes illness

Fact: There is no scientific evidence linking root canal treatment to disease elsewhere in the body. This myth originated from poorly designed research conducted nearly a century ago that has since been debunked.

Aftercare and Recovery

After your root canal treatment:

  • Take prescribed pain medications as needed
  • Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until it's completely restored
  • Continue practicing good oral hygiene including brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups
  • Call our office if you experience severe pain or pressure that lasts more than a few days

Success Rate of Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatments have a high success rate of about 95%, and many teeth fixed with root canal therapy can last a lifetime with proper care.
