Dental Implants

Understanding implants

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a tiny titanium screw that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Over time, it fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. Once stable, an abutment (connector) is attached to the implant, and a natural-looking crown is fitted on top.

The result is a tooth that feels, looks, and functions just like your natural teeth. Unlike dentures, implants don't slip or shift. Unlike bridges, they don't damage adjacent teeth. And unlike tooth extraction, they preserve your jawbone and facial structure.

Is it right for you?

Who benefits from implants

Single missing tooth

Replace one tooth without affecting your neighbouring teeth. Your smile looks complete, and the implant functions like a natural tooth.

Multiple missing teeth

Whether you're missing two teeth or several, we can place multiple implants or use implants to anchor a fixed bridge.

Unstable dentures

If your denture slips or wobbles, implants can stabilise it. Just 2–4 implants can make a dramatic difference in comfort and confidence.

Failed bridges or old restorations

If your old bridge or crown is failing, an implant can provide a long-lasting replacement without affecting other teeth.

Step by step

The implant process

01

Consultation & digital planning

We examine your mouth, take 3D scans, and assess your jawbone. Using advanced imaging, we plan the exact position, angle, and size of your implant for optimal results.

02

Implant placement

Under local anaesthesia, we carefully place the titanium implant into your jawbone. The procedure is quick — usually 20–60 minutes — and you'll be awake and comfortable throughout.

03

Healing & osseointegration

Over the next 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your bone, becoming a permanent part of your jaw. This creates an incredibly strong foundation for your new tooth.

04

Abutment placement

Once healing is complete, we attach the abutment — a small connector — to the top of the implant. This takes just a few minutes and requires minimal discomfort.

05

Crown fitting

We take impressions and create a custom crown that matches your natural teeth perfectly. Once fitted, your implant is ready to use — bite, chew, and smile with confidence.
